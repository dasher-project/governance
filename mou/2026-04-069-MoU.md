The parties:

Stichting NLnet, domiciled in Science Park 400, 1098 XH Amsterdam, The Netherlands, referred to as "NLnet" in this document, represented by [signatory name redacted in public copy — on file with NLnet],

and

W. Wade, [address redacted in public copy — on file with NLnet], United Kingdom, referred to as "Will Wade" in this document,

given that:

- NLnet has the mission "to promote the exchange of electronic information and all that is related or beneficial to that purpose".
- The internet is only as open as the tools people have to use it. For many people with severe speech and motor disabilities, text entry via a regular physical or on-screen keyboard is impossible or impractical, and the assistive technology (AT) that would let them take part fully is often closed, dated, or hard to integrate into the systems they already rely on.
- Dasher is a predictive text entry system, first developed over twenty-five years ago by the Inference Group at the University of Cambridge under the direction of Sir David MacKay, which lets a user write through a continuous gesture rather than discrete key presses. Research and long practical use have shown it to be one of the fastest text entry methods for people typing with eye gaze, a head mouse, or other alternative pointers, and people with disabilities use it to this day. Over time, however, the code base fragmented across platforms and became hard to maintain, and its historic licensing terms discouraged commercial AT companies from integrating and supporting it.
- Will Wade is an assistive technology and augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) specialist, and the lead of Dasher v6 — a comprehensive project that redevelops Dasher on a modern, modular open source code base, with a new next-letter and next-word prediction engine supporting a range of language models, an improved user interface for new users, and documentation and an SDK so that anyone — including commercial AT manufacturers — can integrate Dasher into their own products.
- Will Wade has expressed the intention to contribute to an open internet that includes everyone, by developing this open source software for, and together with, people who cannot use conventional keyboards.
- NLnet thinks that this project is of significant value for the openness and inclusiveness of the internet, and wants to facilitate such a contribution.

agree to the following:

1. **Annex I.** Will Wade has written the proposal "Dasher v6" which is attached to this document as Annex I. Annex I forms an integral part of this Memorandum of Understanding. If and where statements in this annex or other annexes are in contradiction with one or more statements in the main memorandum text, the statement or statements in the main memorandum text will prevail.

2. **Voluntary undertaking; public source.** Will Wade is voluntarily undertaking the project, and is solely responsible for all aspects of the project including planning and coordination as well as involving contributors and partners — as long as such happens in line with the terms and spirit of this MoU. All project work is carried out publicly in the dasher-project organisation on GitHub. The source code of the project shall be made publicly available under the MIT license.

3. **Point of contact.** As the signatory of this MoU, Will Wade shall act as official point of contact in the context of this project; it is the responsibility of Will Wade to notify NLnet in case of any changes.

4. **Reservation.** NLnet commits to make a reservation for the amount of € 50,000 (fifty thousand euros) for Will Wade in order to support the "Dasher v6" project. The reservation is bound to the proposal as contained within Annex I of this MoU.

5. **No consequence on failure.** Should the project fail to complete the goals described in Annex I, partially or in full, there is no other consequence than the termination of this MoU.

6. **Public reporting.** Will Wade commits to publicly report on the progress made within the project on a regular basis (every two months, more often is never a bad thing) and will maintain a public status page for the project to keep the wider internet community informed. As a courtesy, Will Wade may send non-public updates about the status of the project to NLnet, but there is no obligation whatsoever to do so — NLnet is not operationally involved with the project; its only interest is the public benefit that is the result of the project succeeding in reaching its goals.

7. **Claiming donations.** Donations may be claimed up to the reserved amount within a maximum of six months after the proposed end of the project. Donations will be final when the specified milestones or previously agreed partial deliveries have been verified to have been completed. Payments will subsequently be made by wire transfer into a bank account designated by Will Wade.

8. **Public statements and acknowledgement.** NLnet and Will Wade may issue one or more individual or joint public statements announcing the project and NLnet's financial support. Will Wade is also encouraged to visibly and vocally acknowledge NLnet's contribution where possible — e.g. through the project website, promotional materials, in presentations and in source code.

9. **Funding of other contributors.** Funding to any particular group or person will be on the understanding that they coordinate their activities in agreement with Will Wade, in the spirit of cooperation, and in an effort to achieve the results of the "Dasher v6" project.

10. **Not an employment agreement or business contract.** This Memorandum of Understanding cannot be seen as any kind of employment agreement or business contract. NLnet receives no goods or services as a result of this MoU. Any payments are to be made as charitable donations to Will Wade and, where milestones are assigned to them, to other contributors, in the light of a voluntary contribution to the public benefit such as defined within the statutory mission of NLnet foundation. Will Wade is responsible for paying any and all taxes or other fees with regard to this grant, and to inform any relevant authorities within his country of these donations should this be legally required. All payments under this MoU are charitable donations outside the scope of VAT; no VAT will be charged or claimed by any party.

11. **European dimension.** The parties intend that at least 60% of the reserved amount will be donated to contributors domiciled in countries eligible for Horizon Europe funding, including the United Kingdom as an associated country.[^1]

12. **Research and development only.** The reservation funds research and development activities of the project only — including engineering, specifications, bug fixes and documentation. Operational costs, community management, hosting and promotional activities are not funded under this MoU. Travel and hardware costs are not funded as milestones; where genuinely required for the research and development activities (for example participant travel for the user study), they must be agreed with NLnet in advance, with invoices submitted to NLnet ahead of any payment, and will be limited in scale.

13. **Generative AI policy.** The project complies with NLnet's [policy on the use of Generative Artificial Intelligence](https://nlnet.nl/foundation/policies/generativeAI/). Under the transitional arrangement for concluding-phase NGI Zero Commons Fund grants, the project follows version [1.1](https://nlnet.nl/foundation/policies/generativeAI/1.1/) of the policy: generative AI may assist development, with public disclosure of the project's general stance (README), commit-level provenance marking for generated code (model and version used, prompts or a summary), and no purely AI-generated outputs submitted as completed milestones. The project may voluntarily move to version 2.0 (read-only use) should its workflow change.

[^1]: For NLnet's requirements regarding the European dimension, see <https://nlnet.nl/NGI0/eu-dimension/>.

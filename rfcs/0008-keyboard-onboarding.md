# Keyboard extension / IME onboarding (Apple & Android)

> **Withdrawn 2026-11-08 and folded into
> [RFC 0004](./0004-onboarding.md).** The keyboard and IME enablement flow
> is now RFC 0004's "Keyboard and IME enablement" chapter: shared state
> model, Android IME detection and deep-links, the iOS keyboard-extension
> heartbeat flow, copy and translation, and scope boundaries. Its open
> questions moved to RFC 0004's Unresolved questions (items 6-10).
>
> Nothing was lost: this RFC was never implemented, and no shipped
> behaviour changed. Future first-run work happens under RFC 0004.
